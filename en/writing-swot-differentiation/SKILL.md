# SWOT Analysis and Differentiation Strategy Report

## Analysis procedure
- Review all of the provided material closely, then write the SWOT analysis first and the differentiation strategy second.
- Derive each SWOT element directly from evidence in the provided material.
- Derive the differentiation strategy by crossing the SWOT results with the target segment and the campaign requirements.
- Rather than listing items, write about the links between them and their strategic implications.
- Do not search externally; the goal is to interpret the provided information in depth and draw strategic insight.

## Report structure
1. Executive Summary: three to five lines on the core strategic direction
2. SWOT analysis
   - Strengths: our internal strengths relative to competitors
   - Weaknesses: our internal weaknesses relative to competitors
   - Opportunities: opportunities in the target market and the external environment
   - Threats: threats arising from the competitive landscape and the external environment
3. SWOT cross analysis
   - SO strategy (strengths × opportunities): use strengths to seize opportunities
   - ST strategy (strengths × threats): use strengths to defend against threats
   - WO strategy (weaknesses × opportunities): fix weaknesses to seize opportunities
   - WT strategy (weaknesses × threats): minimize weaknesses and threats at the same time
4. Differentiation strategy
   - Core differentiation points: our strengths and value proposition that clearly set us apart from competitors
   - Differentiation by target segment: differentiation messages and strategies tailored to the selected target segments
   - Campaign application: an execution direction that links the differentiation strategy to the campaign requirements

## Output rules
- Output language: the language of the request, format: Markdown
- Write the report in prose (minimize bullet lists)
- Save only the final report to the file (do not output the analysis process)

## Prohibited
- Referring to external information beyond the provided material
- Speculative claims without evidence
- Overlapping content across SWOT items
